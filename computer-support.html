Computer Support

Friendly, local computer support for homes and small businesses.

Overview

Technology faults aren't always obvious. If your computer, printer, Wi-Fi or email has stopped working properly, Dunmow Computer Services can help. Computer Support is the traditional side of DCS: you already have a technology problem, and I help diagnose and fix it.

Whether you need help with a slow laptop, a new computer setup, suspicious pop-ups, printer problems, lost passwords or transferring files, I provide clear, jargon-free support without unnecessary upselling.

How we can help

Computer Repairs & Upgrades

Diagnosis, troubleshooting, performance improvements, memory upgrades and practical advice on whether repair or replacement is the better option.

Virus & Scam Removal

Help removing fake pop-ups, browser scams, malware and other security issues, with advice on staying safer online.

Printers, Wi-Fi & Devices

Setup and troubleshooting for printers, home Wi-Fi, connected devices and everyday technology problems.

Email & Password Help

Support with email setup, account access, password resets and common email problems.

New Computer Setup

Complete setup for new laptops and PCs, including updates, email, Wi-Fi, printers and file transfer.

Backup & Data Transfer

Help transferring photos, documents and important files, plus advice on sensible backup options.

How It Works

  1. Get in touch and briefly explain the problem.
  2. I visit locally or discuss the issue remotely where appropriate.
  3. I diagnose the problem and explain your options in plain English.
  4. If you are happy to proceed, I carry out the work and confirm the outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. DCS offers local home and small business visits in Great Dunmow and surrounding areas.

Can you help if I am not very confident with technology?

Yes. The aim is to explain things clearly and patiently without jargon, so you understand what the issue is and what your options are.

Can you help me set up a new laptop?

Yes. New laptop setup can include updates, email, Wi-Fi, printers, software setup and transferring files from your old computer.

What if the problem cannot be fixed?

If a repair is not practical or cost-effective, I will explain why and help you decide on the best next step.
