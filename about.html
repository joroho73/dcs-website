About Dunmow Computer Services

Friendly, local technology support backed by more than 20 years of professional IT experience.

Meet the founder

Hi, I'm John, founder of Dunmow Computer Services, a local business helping home users and small businesses get the most from their technology. Whether your laptop has stopped working, your Wi-Fi keeps dropping out, your printer refuses to cooperate or you're simply looking for honest, jargon-free advice, I'm here to help.

Before starting DCS, I spent more than twenty years delivering technology, software and data projects for organisations ranging from small businesses to international companies. During that time I learned that technology should make life easier - not more complicated.

Technology That Works For You

My goal is simple: to provide the kind of support I would want myself. That means turning up on time, explaining problems in plain English, recommending sensible solutions and never selling services or equipment that you don't need.

Many of my customers simply want someone they can trust when technology goes wrong. Others want help setting up a new computer, protecting their data, improving Wi-Fi or making sure everything works together reliably. Whatever the problem, I'll always take the time to explain your options before any work is carried out.

Helping Small Businesses Grow

Small businesses often don't have an IT department, yet they rely on technology every day. From email and Microsoft 365 to websites, cloud services and data backups, I help local businesses make better use of the tools they already have while identifying opportunities to improve productivity and reduce unnecessary administration.

Increasingly, that also includes practical Artificial Intelligence. Rather than chasing the latest buzzwords, I focus on real-world AI solutions that save time, automate repetitive tasks and improve the way businesses work.

Local, Independent and Here to Help

Dunmow Computer Services is proudly based in Great Dunmow and supports customers across the local area. As an independent business, every customer matters. You'll deal directly with me from your first enquiry through to the completion of the work, giving you a consistent, personal service rather than being passed between departments.

Whether you need a one-off repair, ongoing technology support or advice about improving your business systems, I'd be delighted to help.
