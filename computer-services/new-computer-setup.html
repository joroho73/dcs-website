Get Your New Computer Ready to Use

Buying a new computer should be an exciting experience, but setting it up can be time-consuming and confusing. From creating accounts and installing updates to transferring years of photographs and documents, there is often more involved than simply switching it on.

Dunmow Computer Services provides a complete new computer setup service for home users and small businesses. I'll configure your new laptop or desktop around your individual needs, helping you make a smooth transition from your old computer.

Initial Computer Setup & Configuration

New computers often come with pre-installed software, trial subscriptions and default settings that may not suit your requirements.

Initial setup can include:

  • Completing the initial Windows or macOS setup.
  • Creating and configuring user accounts.
  • Installing operating system and driver updates.
  • Removing unwanted pre-installed software.
  • Configuring privacy and security settings.
  • Personalising the desktop and system preferences.
  • Connecting your computer to your home or office network.

Your computer will be configured to suit the way you use it, rather than simply accepting the manufacturer's default settings.

Transferring Files From Your Old Computer

One of the biggest concerns when replacing a computer is making sure important files, photographs and personal information are not left behind.

Where your existing data is accessible, I can transfer it from your old computer, external hard drive or cloud storage to your new device.

Data transfer can include:

  • Personal documents and spreadsheets.
  • Photographs, videos and music collections.
  • Desktop files and folders.
  • Browser bookmarks and favourites.
  • Locally stored email and archives, where supported.
  • Files stored on external hard drives and USB devices.

I'll also help organise your files so that you can find them easily on your new computer. Your original data will be retained wherever possible until you are satisfied that everything you need has been transferred.

Software Installation & Configuration

Your new computer may not include the applications you have become accustomed to using. I can install and configure the software you need for everyday tasks, work and leisure.

Common software installations include:

  • Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365.
  • LibreOffice and other free office applications.
  • Google Chrome, Firefox and other web browsers.
  • PDF readers and document management software.
  • Photo editing and management applications.
  • Video calling and communication software.
  • Other specialist applications, subject to compatibility.

Where existing software licences can be transferred, I'll help you do so. If new licences are required, I'll explain the available options, including suitable free alternatives where appropriate.

Email & Online Account Setup

Getting your email working on a new computer can sometimes be more complicated than expected, particularly if you have multiple email accounts or have been using the same email application for many years.

I can help with:

  • Configuring existing email accounts.
  • Setting up Microsoft Outlook and other email applications.
  • Accessing Gmail, Yahoo Mail and other webmail services.
  • Configuring Microsoft, Google and Apple accounts.
  • Transferring contacts and calendars where supported.
  • Setting up password managers and account security.

I'll help you reconnect to your existing services without unnecessarily creating new accounts or purchasing additional subscriptions.

Printers, Scanners & Other Devices

Replacing your computer doesn't necessarily mean replacing your printer, scanner or other accessories. Many existing devices can continue to be used with your new computer.

Device setup can include:

  • Wireless and USB printers.
  • Scanners and multifunction printers.
  • External monitors and docking stations.
  • Wireless keyboards and mice.
  • Webcams, microphones and speakers.
  • External storage and backup devices.

I'll check compatibility, install the necessary drivers and software, and test that your connected devices are working correctly.

Computer Security & Data Backup

Setting up a new computer is an ideal opportunity to review how your personal information and important files are protected.

Security and backup configuration can include:

  • Configuring antivirus and security software.
  • Enabling automatic operating system updates.
  • Setting up secure sign-in options.
  • Reviewing browser security and privacy settings.
  • Configuring local or cloud-based data backups.
  • Setting up external backup drives.

Whether you prefer to keep your files on your computer, use an external backup drive or store them in the cloud, I'll help you choose an approach that suits your needs and explain the advantages and limitations of each option.

Getting Comfortable With Your New Computer

Moving to a new computer or operating system can feel unfamiliar, particularly if you have been using your previous computer for many years.

I'll help you become familiar with your new computer, show you where your files and applications are located, and explain any important differences in plain English.

The aim is to leave you with a computer that is ready to use and that you feel comfortable using, rather than leaving you to work everything out for yourself.
