A reliable internet connection has become an essential part of everyday life. Whether you're working from home, streaming television, making video calls or simply browsing the internet, poor Wi-Fi coverage and unreliable connections can be incredibly frustrating.

Even with a fast broadband package, your internet experience can be affected by the layout of your property, the location of your router, interference from other devices and the limitations of your existing networking equipment.

Common Wi-Fi & Network Problems

Networking problems can affect individual devices, particular rooms or your entire property.

I can help with problems including:

  • Poor Wi-Fi signal strength in certain rooms.
  • Wi-Fi dead spots and limited coverage.
  • Slow or inconsistent internet speeds.
  • Devices frequently disconnecting from Wi-Fi.
  • Buffering when streaming television and video.

  • Connectivity problems following a broadband provider change.

Mesh Wi-Fi Systems

Mesh Wi-Fi systems are an effective way to improve wireless coverage across larger properties, homes with thick walls and buildings with multiple floors.

Unlike traditional Wi-Fi extenders, a mesh system uses multiple access points that work together to provide a coordinated wireless network throughout your property. Compatible devices can move between access points as you move around your home or office.

Benefits of a mesh Wi-Fi system can include:

  • Improved wireless coverage throughout your property.
  • Reduced Wi-Fi dead spots.
  • A single wireless network name and password.
  • Smoother movement between wireless access points.
  • Improved connectivity for multiple devices.
  • The ability to expand coverage with additional mesh units.

I can recommend suitable mesh Wi-Fi equipment, configure it to work with your existing broadband connection and position the individual units to achieve the best practical coverage.

Wi-Fi Extenders & Powerline Adapters

A complete mesh Wi-Fi system is not always necessary. If you only need to improve connectivity in one particular room or area, a Wi-Fi extender or powerline adapter may provide a suitable and more affordable solution.

Wi-Fi Extenders

Wi-Fi extenders receive and retransmit an existing wireless signal to increase its coverage. They can be useful for reaching areas where your router's signal is weak, provided the extender itself receives a sufficiently strong connection.

Powerline Adapters

Powerline adapters use your property's existing electrical wiring to carry network traffic between rooms. Some models provide wired Ethernet connections, while others also provide wireless access.

Powerline technology can be particularly useful where thick walls or other obstacles make wireless connections difficult. Performance depends on the condition and layout of your electrical wiring.

I'll help you understand the advantages and limitations of each technology and recommend the most appropriate solution for your property.

Broadband Speed & Connection Problems

Slow internet access is not necessarily caused by your broadband provider. Your router, wireless signal strength, connected devices and internal network can all affect the speeds you experience.

I can investigate the performance of your broadband connection and local network, helping to establish whether the problem lies within your property or with your internet service provider.

This can include:

  • Testing wired and wireless connection speeds.
  • Assessing Wi-Fi signal strength around your property.
  • Identifying wireless interference and congestion.
  • Reviewing router settings and positioning.
  • Identifying outdated or unsuitable networking equipment.
  • Investigating intermittent connectivity problems.

If the issue appears to be with your broadband service, I'll explain the findings so that you can raise the problem with your provider.

Router Setup & Network Configuration

Your router is at the centre of your home or office network. Correct configuration can improve connectivity, security and compatibility with your other networking equipment.

Router and network configuration services include:

  • Setting up new or replacement broadband routers.
  • Configuring wireless network names and passwords.
  • Optimising wireless channels and frequency bands.
  • Configuring mesh systems and wireless access points.
  • Setting up separate guest Wi-Fi networks.
  • Reviewing network security settings.
  • Connecting existing networking equipment to a new router.

Wired Networking & Ethernet Connections

Although Wi-Fi is convenient, a wired Ethernet connection can provide a more consistent and reliable connection for devices that remain in a fixed location.

Wired networking can be particularly useful for:

  • Desktop computers and home office workstations.
  • Smart televisions and streaming devices.
  • Gaming consoles.
  • Network printers and storage devices.
  • Small business computers and shared equipment.
  • Connecting mesh Wi-Fi units and wireless access points.

I can help configure Ethernet connections, network switches and existing network cabling, as well as advise on suitable equipment and options for extending your wired network.

Wi-Fi for Garden Offices & Outbuildings

Garden offices, workshops and detached buildings often fall outside the effective range of a standard broadband router.

Extending connectivity to these areas may require a different approach from improving Wi-Fi coverage within your home.

Potential solutions include:

  • Additional mesh Wi-Fi units where coverage permits.
  • Powerline networking where electrical wiring is suitable.
  • Outdoor wireless access points.
  • Point-to-point wireless connections between buildings.
  • Dedicated wired network connections.

The most appropriate solution will depend on the distance between buildings, physical obstacles, available electrical connections and your intended use of the network.
