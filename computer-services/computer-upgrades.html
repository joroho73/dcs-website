Get More From Your Existing Computer

Is your computer becoming increasingly slow? Are you running out of storage space or struggling to run several applications at the same time? You may not need to buy a new computer.

Upgrading the right components can make a significant difference to your computer's performance and usability. Dunmow Computer Services can assess your existing laptop or desktop, identify suitable upgrades and recommend the best options based on your needs and budget.

SSD Upgrades

Replacing a traditional mechanical hard drive with a solid-state drive (SSD) is often one of the most effective ways to improve the performance of an older computer.

Benefits of upgrading to an SSD include:

  • Faster computer startup and shutdown times.
  • Quicker application loading.
  • Improved overall responsiveness.
  • Quieter operation with no moving parts.
  • Reduced power consumption in many devices.

Both SATA and M.2 NVMe SSD upgrades are available, depending on your computer's hardware and compatibility. Where possible, your existing operating system, applications and personal files can be transferred to the new drive without having to set up your computer from scratch.

Memory (RAM) Upgrades

If your computer struggles when you have several applications or browser tabs open, insufficient memory may be contributing to the problem.

Increasing your computer's memory can help with:

  • Running multiple applications at the same time.
  • Working with large documents and spreadsheets.
  • Browsing the internet with multiple tabs open.
  • Photo editing and other demanding applications.
  • Reducing slowdowns caused by insufficient memory.

I'll check your computer's existing memory configuration, supported memory type and maximum capacity before recommending an upgrade. Some modern laptops have permanently fitted memory that cannot be upgraded, so compatibility is always checked first.

Storage Capacity Upgrades

Running out of space for your photographs, videos, documents and applications? A larger storage drive can give you more room for your files without replacing your computer.

Storage upgrade options include:

  • Replacing an existing SSD with a higher-capacity model.
  • Replacing a traditional hard drive with a larger SSD.
  • Installing an additional internal storage drive where supported.
  • Transferring existing files and applications to a new drive.

Whether you need more space for everyday documents or a large collection of photographs and videos, I can recommend a storage solution suited to your requirements.

Windows 11 Upgrades & Compatibility

With standard support for Windows 10 having ended in October 2025, it is important to consider whether your existing computer can run a supported operating system.

I can assess your computer's compatibility with Windows 11, including its processor, memory, storage and security requirements.

Where appropriate, hardware upgrades may help prepare your computer for Windows 11. However, not every computer can be made compatible through additional memory or storage alone. I'll explain the available options before you spend money on unnecessary upgrades.

Desktop Computer Hardware Upgrades

Desktop computers often provide more upgrade opportunities than laptops. Depending on the existing hardware, components can be replaced or upgraded to improve performance, capacity and reliability.

Potential desktop upgrades include:

  • Processors (CPUs).
  • Graphics cards (GPUs).
  • Memory and storage.
  • Power supplies.
  • Cooling systems and fans.

Component compatibility, power requirements and the overall condition of your computer will be considered before any upgrades are recommended.

Upgrade Rather Than Replace

A well-chosen upgrade can extend the useful life of a computer by several years. It can also be considerably less expensive than purchasing a replacement and transferring everything to a new device.

Keeping existing equipment in use for longer also helps reduce electronic waste and the environmental impact associated with manufacturing new computers.

Not every computer is worth upgrading, however. I'll provide practical, independent advice based on the age, condition and capabilities of your equipment, helping you decide whether upgrading or replacing it offers better value.
