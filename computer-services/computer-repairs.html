Should You Repair or Replace Your Computer?

A faulty or slow computer doesn't necessarily need replacing. In many cases, repairing or upgrading an existing device can extend its useful life at a fraction of the cost of buying a new one.

I'll consider the age, condition and performance of your computer alongside the likely cost of repair. If replacing the device makes more financial sense, I'll tell you.

Repairing and maintaining existing equipment can also help reduce electronic waste and unnecessary spending.
