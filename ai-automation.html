A closer look

What if routine customer emails could draft themselves?

Imagine a customer asking whether a venue has parking, whether a course is suitable for beginners or what time check-in starts. The answer may already be on your website, but someone still has to find it and write a reply.

An AI-assisted workflow can search approved information, prepare a relevant response and flag anything it cannot answer confidently.

Start with drafts, not automatic sending. Your team can review responses while the process is tested. More automation can be considered when reliability has been demonstrated.

Frequently asked questions

Questions businesses often ask

Do I need to replace my existing software?

Usually not. Many automations work by connecting compatible tools you already use. We first check whether the systems offer suitable integrations and access controls.

Will AI send messages without anyone checking them?

Not unless that is explicitly agreed and appropriate. A sensible starting point is to prepare drafts for human review, then consider selective automation after testing.

Is my business too small for automation?

Not necessarily. Even a few recurring tasks can add up, but the cost of the solution should be proportionate to the time or errors it saves.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the task, the systems involved and the level of testing and support required. After an initial discussion, we'll explain the options and provide a clear proposal before work begins.

What happens to customer data?

We identify which information the process needs, where it is sent and who can access it. Data protection and appropriate agreements are considered before any live customer data is used.
