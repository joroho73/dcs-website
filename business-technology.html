Overview

Technology should help your business grow, not get in the way. Many small businesses rely on a collection of software, online services and manual processes that have evolved over time. They work, but often not as efficiently as they could.

Dunmow Computer Services helps you review the technology you're already using, identify opportunities for improvement and recommend practical solutions that make everyday work simpler, faster and more reliable.

Whether you're a sole trader, retailer, consultant or growing business, my aim is to help you spend less time managing technology and more time running your business.

Do I need to replace all my existing software?

Usually not. The first step is understanding what you already have and making better use of it before considering new solutions.

Is this only for larger businesses?

No. These services are designed specifically for sole traders, home offices and small businesses that don't have an in-house IT department.

Will you recommend AI?

Only where it genuinely solves a business problem. AI is one of many tools available, and sometimes a simpler solution is the better choice.

Can you implement the changes you recommend?

Yes. I can help with setup, migration, configuration and ongoing support where required.
